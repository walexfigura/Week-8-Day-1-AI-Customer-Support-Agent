# Shipping Times FAQ

## Purpose

Helps customers estimate when an order will arrive.

## Quick answer

Delivery time = processing (**1–2 business days**) + transit. Standard transit is
**3–7 business days** (free on orders $50+, otherwise $5.95). Express is **1–3
business days** ($14.95). Alaska and Hawaii: Standard only, **5–10 business days**.

## Detailed instructions

Example: an order placed Monday morning with Standard shipping usually ships
Monday or Tuesday and arrives between the following Thursday and the Wednesday
after that. Weekends and US federal holidays don't count as business days.

## Common customer questions

- *"Do you ship to Canada?"* — Not at this time; US addresses only.
- *"Can I upgrade to Express after ordering?"* — Only before the order ships, and
  only a team member can change it.
- *"Can Express go to a P.O. box?"* — No, Express needs a street address.

## Relevant exceptions

- Severe weather or carrier disruptions can add delays.

## When to escalate

- Upgrade or address-change requests (order modification).
