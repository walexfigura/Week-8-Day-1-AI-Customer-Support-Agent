# Tracking Information FAQ

## Purpose

Explains tracking numbers, carrier scans, and what to do when tracking looks wrong.

## Quick answer

You'll get a tracking email as soon as your order ships. New tracking numbers
can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. If tracking hasn't moved for
5 business days, we open a trace with the carrier.

## Detailed instructions

- **"Label created" / no scans yet:** the carrier hasn't scanned the package; wait
  up to 24 hours.
- **"In transit":** the package is moving. Scans can be a day or two apart.
- **"Delivery exception" / weather delay:** the carrier is delayed; most packages
  resume within a few days.
- **"Delivered" but you don't have it:** wait 48 hours (packages are sometimes scanned
  early), check around your property, with neighbors, and any mailroom. If it still
  hasn't appeared after 48 hours, contact us and we'll open a claim.
- **Two tracking numbers:** your items shipped in separate packages.

## Common customer questions

- *"My tracking hasn't changed in three days."* — Usually normal. After 5 business
  days without movement, we open a carrier trace.
- *"My package is late."* — Packages 10 business days past the estimated date are
  treated as lost, and we'll review a replacement or refund.

## Relevant exceptions

- Packages to Alaska and Hawaii may go several days between scans.

## When to escalate

- No movement for 5+ business days, presumed lost, or delivered-not-received
  after 48 hours.
