# Order Status FAQ

## Purpose

Explains what each order status means, in customer-friendly language.

## Quick answer

You can check any order's status with your order number (the four-digit number
in your confirmation email, e.g. #1042). Most orders ship within 1–2 business
days of being placed.

## Detailed instructions

| Status customers may see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Unfulfilled / Processing** | We've received the order and are packing it. |
| **Partially fulfilled** | Some items shipped; the rest will ship separately (often a backorder). |
| **Fulfilled / Shipped** | Everything has been handed to the carrier; tracking is available. |
| **In transit** | The carrier is moving the package. |
| **Delivered** | The carrier recorded delivery. |
| **Refunded / Partially refunded** | Some or all of the order was refunded. |

To protect privacy, we can only share order details with the person who placed
the order, writing from the email address used at checkout.

## Common customer questions

- *"I placed my order last night — has it shipped?"* — Probably not yet; orders ship
  within 1–2 business days. You'll get a tracking email when it does.
- *"Can I check on my friend's order?"* — We can only discuss an order with the
  person who placed it.
- *"I don't have my order number."* — It's in the confirmation email subject line.
  Reply from the email you used at checkout and include the number.

## Relevant exceptions

- Backordered items show the expected restock date on the order.

## When to escalate

- The customer can't access their confirmation email *and* can't verify identity
  (Support Lead).
- Requests to change or cancel an order.
