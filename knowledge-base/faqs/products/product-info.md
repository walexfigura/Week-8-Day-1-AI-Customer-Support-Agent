## Common customer questions

- *"Is the bottle dishwasher safe?"* — The lid is top-rack dishwasher safe; hand-wash
  the body to protect the vacuum seal.
- *"Will the bottle fit my car's cup holder?"* — The base is 2.9 in (74 mm) wide and
  fits most standard cup holders.
- *"Can I charge the lamp with my phone charger?"* — Yes, any USB-C adapter rated
  5V/2A or higher.
- *"Are the earbuds waterproof?"* — They're IPX4 sweat- and splash-resistant, not
  waterproof.
