# Product Troubleshooting FAQ

## Purpose

Step-by-step fixes for the most common product issues, and when to stop
troubleshooting and start a return or warranty claim.

## Quick answer

Most Lumen Desk Lamp issues (won't turn on, flickering) are caused by a low
battery or an underpowered charger: charge for 2 hours with a 5V/2A USB-C adapter,
then reset by holding the power button for 10 seconds. Most Nimbus Earbuds pairing
issues are fixed by a reset in the case.

## Detailed instructions

### Lumen Rechargeable Desk Lamp — won't turn on

1. Charge for at least **2 hours** using the included cable and a USB-C adapter
   rated **5V/2A** or higher (laptop ports may be too weak).
2. The charging light should glow amber, then turn green when full.
3. If there's no charging light, try a different cable and outlet.
4. Reset: hold the power button for **10 seconds** until the light blinks twice.

### Lumen Rechargeable Desk Lamp — flickering

1. Flickering below **10% battery** is normal low-battery behavior. Charge fully.
2. Flickering while plugged in usually means the adapter supplies less than 5V/2A.
   Switch to a 5V/2A adapter.
3. Reset: hold the power button for 10 seconds.
4. If it still flickers when fully charged on a 5V/2A adapter, it's likely a
   defect: start a return (within 30 days of delivery) or a warranty claim
   (within 12 months).

### Nimbus Wireless Earbuds — won't pair or one side silent

1. Put both earbuds in the case and close the lid for 10 seconds.
2. Remove "Nimbus" from your phone's Bluetooth list.
3. Open the case and hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes white.
4. Pair again from your phone's Bluetooth settings.

### Trailhead Insulated Bottle — not keeping drinks cold

1. Make sure the lid's gasket is seated evenly.
2. Pre-chill with ice water for 5 minutes.
3. If the outside of the bottle sweats or feels cold, the vacuum seal has failed —
   that's a warranty issue (24 months).

## Safety — stop troubleshooting

If any device becomes **hot to the touch, swells, smells like burning, smokes, or
sparks**, stop using it, unplug it, and contact us. This is escalated urgently to
the Warranty Team. Do not attempt further troubleshooting.

## Common customer questions

- *"My lamp flickers sometimes."* — Usually low battery or a weak adapter; see above.
- *"Is it normal for the lamp to get warm while charging?"* — Slightly warm is normal;
  hot to the touch is not — stop using it.

## Relevant exceptions

- Physical damage (cracks, dents) isn't fixable by troubleshooting — see the return
  policy (damaged items) or warranty policy.

## When to escalate

- Any safety symptom (urgent).
- Steps completed and the issue persists → return or warranty claim.
