# Exchange Process FAQ

## Purpose

Explains how to swap an item for a different color or size of the same product.

## Quick answer

Exchanges are available within **30 days of delivery** for the same product in a
different color or size. Start in the returns portal and choose "Exchange". Your
first exchange has a free return label.

## Detailed instructions

1. Open the returns portal and enter your order number and email.
2. Choose the item and select **Exchange**, then the new color or size.
3. Print the free label and drop the package off.
4. Your replacement ships as soon as the carrier scans your return — you don't
   have to wait for it to reach our warehouse.

## Common customer questions

- *"Can I exchange for a different product?"* — Not directly. Return the item (refund)
  and place a new order.
- *"The color I want is out of stock."* — You can wait for restock or return for a refund.

## Relevant exceptions

- Final-sale items, gift cards, and opened Nimbus Wireless Earbuds can't be exchanged.

## When to escalate

- Exchange requests outside the 30-day window (exception — Support Lead).
