# Refund Timeline FAQ

## Purpose

Explains when customers can expect to see a refund.

## Quick answer

After the warehouse receives your return, we issue the refund within **5 business
days** to your original payment method. Your bank then takes **3–10 business days**
to post it.

## Detailed instructions

The refund journey:

1. You start a return in the returns portal and ship it with the prepaid label.
2. The carrier delivers it to our warehouse (usually 3–7 business days).
3. The warehouse inspects it and we issue the refund within 5 business days.
4. You get a refund confirmation email.
5. Your bank posts it within 3–10 business days.

Change-of-mind returns have a **$6.95 label fee** deducted from the refund. Damaged,
defective, or wrong items are refunded in full with no label fee.

## Common customer questions

- *"It's been a week since I shipped it back."* — The return may still be in transit
  or being inspected. Check the return tracking number.
- *"You said my refund was issued but I don't see it."* — Banks can take up to 10
  business days after the refund date.
- *"Can you refund me to a different card?"* — No, refunds always go to the original
  payment method.

## Relevant exceptions

- Refunds for lost packages don't require a return.

## When to escalate

- More than 10 business days have passed since the refund was issued.
- The customer disputes the refund amount (Billing).
