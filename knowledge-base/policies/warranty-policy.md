# Limited Warranty Policy

> **Authoritative policy.** Warranty periods per product also appear on each
> product record as `warranty_months`; they must match this document.

## Purpose

Explains what Northstar Supply's limited warranty covers, for how long, and how
customers make a claim.

## Quick answer

Northstar products are covered against manufacturing defects: **12 months** for
electronics (Lumen Rechargeable Desk Lamp, Nimbus Wireless Earbuds), **24 months**
for the Trailhead Insulated Bottle, and **90 days** for everything else. The
warranty doesn't cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear.

## Detailed policy

### 1. Coverage periods (from the delivery date)

| Product | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Lumen Rechargeable Desk Lamp (`NS-LMP-01`) | 12 months |
| Nimbus Wireless Earbuds (`NS-EAR-02`) | 12 months |
| Trailhead Insulated Bottle 24 oz (`NS-BTL-24`) | 24 months |
| All other products | 90 days |
| Gift cards | Not applicable |

### 2. What's covered

- Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use: e.g. a lamp that won't
  hold a charge, a bottle that stops insulating, earbuds where one side stops working.

### 3. What's not covered

- Accidental damage (drops, spills on electronics), misuse, unauthorized repairs.
- Normal wear such as scratches, fading, or pilling.
- Lost or stolen items.

### 4. Making a claim

1. Provide the order number, a short description, and a photo or video of the issue.
2. The **Warranty Team** reviews the claim and decides the remedy: repair,
   replacement, or store credit.
3. Remedies are decided by people. Support agents and the AI assistant cannot
   promise a specific remedy.

### 5. Relationship to returns

- Within 14 days of delivery, a damaged or defective item is handled under the
  Return Policy (replacement or refund).
- After 14 days, defects are handled under this warranty.

## Common customer questions

- *"My lamp stopped charging after 4 months."* — Covered (12-month electronics
  warranty). Start a claim with the order number and a short video.
- *"I dropped my bottle and it dented."* — Accidental damage isn't covered.

## Relevant exceptions

- Safety issues (overheating, swelling batteries) are handled urgently regardless
  of warranty status.

## When to escalate

- Every warranty claim goes to the Warranty Team (normal priority).
- Safety issues → urgent.
