# Shipping and Delivery Policy

> **Authoritative policy.** The numeric values in this file's header are read by
> the application to calculate each shipment's `delivery_assessment`.

## Purpose

Explains how quickly Northstar Supply ships orders, delivery estimates, and
what happens when a package is late, stalled, lost, or marked delivered but missing.

## Quick answer

Orders ship within **1–2 business days**. Standard shipping takes **3–7 business
days** after shipment and is free on orders of $50 or more. Express takes **1–3
business days**. If a package hasn't moved in 5 business days, we open a carrier
trace; if it's 10 business days past its estimated delivery date, we treat it as
lost and offer a replacement or refund after review.

## Detailed policy

### 1. Where we ship

- All 50 US states. We do not ship internationally.
- Alaska and Hawaii: Standard shipping only, **5–10 business days**.
- Express shipping is not available to P.O. boxes.

### 2. Processing

- Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within **1–2 business days**.
- Orders placed before 12:00 p.m. Eastern on a business day usually ship the same day.
- Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding US federal holidays.

### 3. Shipping methods and costs

| Method | Transit after shipment | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (UPS Ground or USPS Ground Advantage) | 3–7 business days | Free on orders $50+, otherwise $5.95 |
| Express (UPS 2nd Day Air or similar) | 1–3 business days | $14.95 |

### 4. Tracking

- A tracking email is sent when the order ships.
- New tracking numbers can take up to 24 hours to show the first carrier scan.
- Orders with items shipping separately get one tracking number per shipment.

### 5. Backorders and partial shipments

- If an item is temporarily out of stock, available items ship first at no extra
  cost and the remaining item ships when restocked. The expected restock date is
  shown on the order.

### 6. Late and stalled packages

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Past the estimated delivery date | The package is **late**. Most late packages arrive within a few days. |
| No tracking movement for **5 business days** | Support opens a **carrier trace**. |
| **10 business days** past the estimated delivery date | The package is **presumed lost**. The customer may choose a replacement or a full refund, subject to human approval. |

### 7. Delivered but not received

- Carriers sometimes mark a package delivered shortly before it arrives. Customers
  should wait **48 hours** after the delivered scan and check around the property,
  with neighbors, and with any building mailroom.
- After 48 hours, support files a carrier claim and a replacement or refund is
  reviewed by a team member.

### 8. Address changes and cancellations

- Possible only before the order is fulfilled, and only by a team member.

## Common customer questions

- *"When will my order arrive?"* — Processing (1–2 business days) plus transit time
  for the shipping method.
- *"Why do I have two tracking numbers?"* — Items shipped separately.
- *"Tracking hasn't updated in days."* — See "Late and stalled packages."

## Relevant exceptions

- Severe weather and carrier disruptions can add delays; the late-package timeline
  still applies.
- Shipping fees are refunded when a package is presumed lost.

## When to escalate

- Package presumed lost, stalled 5+ business days, or delivered-not-received after 48 hours.
- Any request to change an address or cancel an order.
