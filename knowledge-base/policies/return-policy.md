# Return and Refund Policy

> **Authoritative policy.** FAQs and procedures summarize this document. If any
> other document disagrees, this one wins. The values in this file's header are
> read by the Python application, so the policy text and the code cannot drift apart.

## Purpose

Defines which Northstar Supply purchases can be returned or exchanged, how
refunds are issued, and which situations require a human decision.

## Quick answer

Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** if they are unused and
in original condition. Start a return in the self-service returns portal. Refunds
go back to the original payment method within **5 business days** after the
warehouse receives the item; banks then take **3–10 business days** to post it.

## Detailed policy

### 1. Return window

- Customers may start a return up to **30 calendar days after the delivery date**
  shown in tracking. Day 1 is the day after delivery.
- The return must be *started* within the window; the package may be scanned by the
  carrier up to 7 days later.

### 2. Condition

- Items must be unused, clean, and in original condition.
- Electronics (Lumen Rechargeable Desk Lamp, Nimbus Wireless Earbuds) must include
  all accessories and original packaging.

### 3. Items that cannot be returned

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Northstar Digital Gift Card (`NS-GIFT-50`) | Never returnable or refundable. |
| Clearance / final-sale items, currently the Harbor Ceramic Mug Set (`NS-MUG-4C`) | Final sale. Not returnable unless damaged on arrival. |
| Nimbus Wireless Earbuds (`NS-EAR-02`) | Returnable **only if unopened**, for hygiene reasons. Opened earbuds are covered by the warranty if defective. |

### 4. Damaged, defective, or wrong items

- Report within **14 days of delivery**, with a photo when possible.
- The customer chooses a free replacement or a full refund; no label fee applies.
- A Northstar team member must authorize the replacement or refund.
- Defects reported after 14 days are handled under the Warranty Policy.

### 5. Return shipping

- Change-of-mind returns: prepaid label, **$6.95 label fee** deducted from the refund.
- Damaged, defective, or wrong items and first exchanges: free label.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the problem was our error.

### 6. Refunds

- Issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** of the warehouse
  receiving the return.
- Banks and card issuers typically take **3–10 business days** to post the refund.
- Refunds are never issued in cash or to a different payment method.

### 7. Exchanges

- Same product, different color or size, within the 30-day window.
- Start in the returns portal and choose "Exchange". The replacement ships as soon
  as the carrier scans the return package.

### 8. Exceptions

- Returns outside the window or of final-sale items require approval from the
  **Support Lead**. Exceptions are rare and are never promised in advance.
- Refunds or replacements for lost or undelivered packages follow the Shipping
  Policy and also require human approval.

## Common customer questions

- *"Can I return something I bought 5 weeks ago?"* — Not under the standard policy;
  the window is 30 days from delivery. If the item is defective, check the warranty.
- *"Do I pay for return shipping?"* — $6.95 for change-of-mind returns; free for
  damaged, defective, or wrong items and first exchanges.
- *"Can I get a refund on a gift card?"* — No, gift cards are non-refundable.

## Relevant exceptions

- Items damaged on arrival are refundable even if they are final sale, when reported
  within 14 days.
- Orders refunded because of a lost package do not require the item to be returned.

## When to escalate

- Any refund, replacement, or exception request → human approval required.
- Safety concerns with a product → urgent escalation (see escalation guide).
